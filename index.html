About Us

Amistad provides bilingual in‑home childcare with a strong focus on emotional development, independence, and hands‑on learning. We believe children thrive when they feel supported, encouraged, and safe.

Curriculum

Creative Play

Art, music, stories, and pretend play encourage imagination and self-expression.

Hands-On Learning

Children explore language, numbers, shapes, sorting, and problem-solving through play.

Movement and Outdoors

Movement games and outdoor exploration build coordination, confidence, and curiosity.

Social Growth

Children practice kindness, communication, sharing, and independence in a supportive setting.
