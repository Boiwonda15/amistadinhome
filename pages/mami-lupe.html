About Mami Lupe

I am a mom to five adult children, but most importantly I have 15 grandkids and one great-grandchild! During my free time I love to cook and try new recipes. I am always in the kitchen; it's my favorite place to be!

When I am not in the kitchen, I am at school learning English! I also love to garden. I grow vegetables, herbs, fruits, and flowers, and I am always adding to it!
