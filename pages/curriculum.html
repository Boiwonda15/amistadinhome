Seven Ways Children Grow and Learn

We focus on the seven Early Learning domains of child development:

Social and Emotional

Understanding and managing emotions, sharing, and developing a positive self-image.

Approaches to Learning

Focusing on curiosity, creativity, problem-solving, and experiencing new things.

Health and Physical Development

Developing gross and fine motor skills, coordination, and simple self-help habits.

Language and Literacy

Enhancing listening, speaking, reading, and writing foundations in both English and Spanish.

Mathematics

Exploring numbers, patterns, spatial awareness, and counting.

Science

Learning reasoning and understanding through exploration of the natural and physical world.

Creative Arts

Expressing themselves through music, art, dance, and imaginative play.

Food and Nutrition

Mealtimes are a chance to build healthy routines and practice independence. Families can discuss food preferences, allergies, and dietary needs with us before care begins.

Potty Training

We partner with families during potty training by following the child's readiness, keeping routines calm, and using consistent encouragement between home and daycare.

Growing Goals

Confidence

Build independence through small choices, helpful routines, and celebrating progress.

Communication

Support language development, listening, and expressing feelings with care.

Curiosity

Encourage children to ask questions, investigate, and enjoy discovering something new.
