Growing every day

Curriculum: Learning Through Play and Discovery

Thoughtful experiences that help children explore, create, and grow.

Daily Schedule

Each day balances welcoming routines, active play, quiet learning, meals, rest, and time outdoors. The schedule can be adjusted to support each child's needs and energy.

Activities

Creative Play

Art, music, stories, and pretend play encourage imagination and self-expression.

Hands-On Learning

Children practice early language, numbers, shapes, sorting, and problem-solving through play.

Movement and Outdoors

Movement games and outdoor exploration help children build coordination and confidence.

Social Growth

Children learn kindness, sharing, communication, and independence in a supportive setting.

Food and Nutrition

Mealtimes are a chance to build healthy routines and practice independence. Families can discuss food preferences, allergies, and dietary needs with us before care begins.

Potty Training

We partner with families during potty training by following the child's readiness, keeping routines calm, and using consistent encouragement between home and daycare.

Growing Goals

Confidence

Build independence through small choices, helpful routines, and celebrating progress.

Communication

Support language development, listening, and expressing feelings with care.

Curiosity

Encourage children to ask questions, investigate, and enjoy discovering something new.
