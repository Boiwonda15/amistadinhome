Our Story

Amistad was created to give children a warm, supportive place to learn and grow. Our in-home setting is built around the belief that children do their best learning when they feel safe, encouraged, and known.

Through bilingual care, hands-on experiences, and close family communication, we work to make each child's day meaningful. We celebrate small steps, nurture independence, and create room for every child to develop at their own pace.
