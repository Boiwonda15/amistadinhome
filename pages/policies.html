Amistad Policies

Clear, family-friendly guidelines help create a safe, respectful, and dependable daycare experience.

Basic Policies

Health and Wellness

Families should keep children home when they are ill or may be contagious. Please share health updates, allergies, and medication instructions with us promptly.

Safety and Supervision

Children are supervised throughout the day in a clean, child-friendly environment. Emergency contact information should always be current.

Pickup and Release

Children may only be released to an authorized adult listed by the family. Please notify us ahead of time about schedule or pickup changes.

Positive Guidance

We use calm communication, encouragement, and age-appropriate limits to help children practice kindness, confidence, and self-regulation.

Family Communication

Open communication matters. Families are encouraged to ask questions, share updates, and discuss changes that may affect their child's day.

Payment and Assistance

Tuition schedules, payment expectations, and third-party assistance arrangements will be reviewed with families before care begins.

Questions

Policies can be reviewed together during enrollment. Please contact Amistad with questions or to discuss your family's needs.
